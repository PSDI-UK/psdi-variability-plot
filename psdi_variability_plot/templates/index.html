This service enables you to produce plots that describe the reproducibility of any numerical reaction outcome. The generated scatterplot displays the mean value of the outcome (horizontal bar) and the associated confidence interval (shaded area).

Measurements

Enter a minimum of three values for the reaction outcome, from repeating the experimental process each time under identical conditions. The width of the confidence interval that accompanies the plotted mean value is based on a combination of the outcome variance and the number of data points. To minimise the uncertainty associated with your reaction outcome, it is recommended that at least five measurements are recorded.
